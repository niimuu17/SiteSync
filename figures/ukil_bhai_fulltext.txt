Abstract
Over 80% of citizens in Bangladesh avoid pursuing formal legal remedies for common disputes
due to cost barriers, complex terminology, and unfamiliar procedural requirements.Ukil Bhaiis an
enterprise-grade native Android legal companion engineered to democratize statutory justice. The
system features a deterministic on-deviceRetrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)pipeline
backed by the Bangladesh Penal Code, CrPC, Negotiable Instruments Act 1881, and Consumer
Rights Protection Act 2009. It introduces a three-tier legal triage system, automated administrative
complaint drafting, motion-triggered emergency SOS foreground services, and an advocate chamber
workspace. The persistence layer implements a multi-tier hybrid architecture combining cloud services
(Firebase Authentication,Cloud Firestore,Firebase Realtime Database, andFirebase
Cloud Storagefor legal PDF binaries) with an on-deviceSQLite FTS5 Local Storageengine for
offline statutory indexing. This report details the system architecture, RAG design, database schemas,
and Android component implementations accompanied by production verification screenshots.
1 Introduction & Problem Motivation
Access to statutory justice in developing legal jurisdictions like Bangladesh is constrained by three
critical friction points:
1. The Jargon & Information Barrier:Primary statutes (e.g., The Code of Criminal Procedure
1898 and The Penal Code 1860) remain opaque to ordinary citizens. Citizens frequently struggle
to assess whether a dispute is civil, cognizable, or compoundable.
2. The Consultation Cost Barrier:Routine disputes–such as residential tenancy disagreements,
unpaid wages, and dishonored banking cheques–often escalate because citizens cannot afford
preliminary legal advice from certified advocates.
3. The Procedural Template Barrier:Filing a formal General Diary (GD) with the police or
a complaint with the Directorate of National Consumer Rights Protection (DNCRP) requires
specific statutory terminology that non-specialists cannot draft independently.
Ukil Bhaisolves these barriers by functioning as a complete citizen companion and advocate
workspace. For citizens, it translates complex statutory clauses into plain Bengali, issues actionable triage
guidance, and auto-drafts administrative complaints. For advocates, it digitizes chamber consultations
with real-time briefing queues and client chat rooms.
2 The Legal RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Pipeline
General-purpose Large Language Models (LLMs) frequently fabricate legal provisions or cite Indian,
Pakistani, or British legal sections when queried in Bengali. To guarantee statutory accuracy, Ukil Bhai
implements a deterministic2-Tier Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Hybrid RAG)
architecture combining on-device statutory grounding with live web search:
• Tier 1: On-Device Verified Statutory Corpus:Curated primary statutes (Penal Code 1860,
CrPC 1898, NI Act 1881, Consumer Rights 2009, Rent Control 1991). Guarantees zero latency
and mathematical guardrails against hallucinated penalties.
• Tier 2: Dynamic Live Web Grounding:Powered byBraveSearchClient.java. When the
web toggle is enabled inChatActivity, the app dispatches HTTPS GET queries toapi.search.
brave.com, retrieves the top 3 live Bangladesh legal gazettes and Supreme Court precedents, and
injects them under[Live Legal Web Search Context].

2.1 Step-by-Step Hybrid Execution
1. Query Preprocessing:Normalizes Bengali characters, converts numerals, and isolates procedural
keywords (e.g.,Bhara(Rent),Cheque,Protarona(Fraud),GD).
2. Dual Retrieval & Routing:Matches intent to governing acts. In parallel, if the chat toggle is
active,BraveSearchClient.javaissues an HTTP GET query toapi.search.brave.com.
3. Prompt Augmentation:Injects exact section numbers, statutory limitation windows, punish-
ment maximums, and live web snippets directly into the prompt context.
4. LLM Inference:Dispatches the multi-source augmented payload via TLS 1.3 to Groq Cloud
(qwen/qwen-2.5-32b) or Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.
5. Client-Side Rendering:The mobile UI renders triage badges, statutory citation pills, clickable
external web citations, and emergency dialer links.

3.1 Core Modules & Exclusive Engineering Capabilities
Each module incorporates specialized, production-hardened engineering solutions:
1. Dual-Tier RAG Legal Chat & Multimodal Vision Analyzer (ChatActivity.java):Fea-
tures an integrated multimodal document reader allowing citizens to snap live camera photos or
select gallery images of court summons, legal notices, or eviction letters. The analyzer implements
a dual-processing pipeline: cloud-level multimodal vision (Google Gemini Flash) with instant offline
fallback to on-deviceGoogle ML Kit Text Recognition. Every query is classified into a 3-tier color
triagesystem: Green (Self-Remedy), Amber (Mediation), orRed (Advocate Mandatory).
Interactive in-text phone numbers (e.g., 16430, 999) auto-launch Intent.ACTION_DIAL, physical
court locations launch Google Maps with routed navigation, and in-line citation pills open a modal
StatuteDetailBottomSheetwithout losing chat scroll position.
2. Automated Administrative Complaint Drafter (AdminWizardActivity.java):A 4-step
guided draftsman generating formal legal complaints conforming to statutory standards: Thana General
Diaries under CrPC Section 44 and Police Regulations Bengal (PRB) Rule 377, and Consumer Rights
complaints under Section 76 of the Consumer Rights Protection Act 2009. The module features a
One-Tap Government Email Dispatcherthat pre-fills the official recipient (nccc@dncrp.com),
statutory subject line, and complete Bengali complaint text, automatically attaching the generated
PDF binary via Android’s secureFileProvider. Additionally, an integrated NI Act 138 countdown
calculator tracks mandatory statutory limitation windows (30-day notice, 30-day grace, and court filing
deadlines).
3. Case Brief Handoff Pipeline (CitizenConsultationsActivity.java):Empowers citizens to
forward AI-triaged legal consultation briefs directly from the chat 3-dot overflow menu to a selected

advocate’s chamber inbox. Consultation briefs are formatted with rich Markdown tables, statutory

4. Multi-Tier Security & Privacy Architecture:Engineered for strict attorney-client privi-

5. Bangla Legal Dictionary & 2D DP OBST Engine ( LegalDictionaryActivity.java):
Houses 114+ statutory legal terms. Operates a hybrid search architecture: an in-memory Prefix Trie for
instant character-by-character autocomplete alongside an Optimal Binary Search Tree (OBST). The
OBST is computed via 2D Dynamic Programming with Knuth’s Monotonicity Optimization (O(N2)),
weighting terms by citizen query frequencies to minimize expected search depth. An academic diagnostic
sheet (dialog_obst_metrics.xml) displays live depth levels, root node keys, and tree access costs.
6. Cloud Document Vault (VaultActivity.java & PdfVaultManager.java):Provides an en-
crypted on-device and cloud-synced document repository. Generated PDF complaint binaries are cached
locally and uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage underusers/{uid}/pdfs/, with metadata indexed
in Cloud Firestore collectiongenerated_documents. Supports offline caching, document viewing via
securecontent://URIs, and native Android print spooler management.
7. Emergency Foreground SOS Service (LegalSosService.java):Continuously monitors ac-
celerometer shake vectors (g-force> 2.4g or > 12.5 m/s2). When triggered, it promotes itself to an
Android Foreground Service with an ongoing notification tray alert (START_STICKY). The service simul-
taneously records ambient audio evidence viaMediaRecorder and dispatches GPS distress coordinates
via SMS to designated emergency contacts even while the device is locked.

4.2 Multi-Tier Database Architecture (Quad-Firebase Cloud & On-Device SQLite)
• Firebase Authentication (com.google.firebase:firebase-auth):Manages encrypted token
issuance and session persistence, routing advocates toLawyerDashboardActivity and citizens to
MainActivity.
• Cloud Firestore (com.google.firebase:firebase-firestore):Primary NoSQL document
database:
–users: Citizen profile attributes and emergency contacts.
–lawyers: Advocate credentials, bar affiliations, and rates.
–consultation_requests: Incoming citizen case briefs (PENDING,ACCEPTED,DECLINED).
–chat_rooms: Real-time 1-on-1 direct consultation metadata and message sub-collections.
–generated_documents: Legal document metadata and vault histories.
• Firebase Realtime Database (com.google.firebase:firebase-database):Powers the live
online/offline presence system (RealtimePresenceManager.java) using .info/connected and
native onDisconnect() handlers. This ensures green status indicators turn off immediately if a
device experiences battery exhaustion or network failure.
• Firebase Cloud Storage (com.google.firebase:firebase-storage):Securely hosts gener-
ated PDF legal complaint binaries (Thana GDs, DNCRP forms, and case briefs) under cloud bucket
path users/{uid}/pdfs/{documentId}.pdf. Implemented through PdfVaultManager.java,
which generates cryptographic tokenized download URLs and stores them in Cloud Firestore
collectiongenerated_documents.

5.2 System Services & Receivers
• Foreground Emergency SOS Service (LegalSosService.java):Triggered by an accelerome-
ter shake vector (> 12.5 m/s2). Elevates to a Foreground Service with an ongoing notification
tray alert ("Emergency Recording Active"), records ambient audio evidence viaMediaRecorder,
and dispatches GPS distress SMS messages to designated contacts.
• Quick Settings Tile Service (QuickGdTileService.java):Extends Android’s TileService,
integrating an instant General Diary draftsman button directly into the Android notification
pull-down shade.
• Broadcast Receivers:Dynamically registered AirplaneModeReceiver intercepts broadcast
event ACTION_AIRPLANE_MODE_CHANGED to pause network calls and display an offline banner.
NetworkConnectivityReceiverhandles cellular/WiFi transitions.
• Content Providers ( FileProvider):Defined in AndroidManifest.xml with authority
${applicationId}.fileprovider. Exposes securecontent:// URIs with temporary read grants
for external PDF sharing.
